Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026 • Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) & IT Act 2000

1. Our Core Privacy Philosophy: Zero-Cloud Hostage

At Bill 2 Bharat Technologies ("Bill 2 Bharat", "we", "our", or "us"), we build offline-first operating systems for real Indian businesses (BharatRx™, KrishiBharat™, ArogyaBharat™, RasoiBharat™, and NirmanBharat™).

Unlike generic cloud billing software providers that store your customer Khata, patient prescriptions, and turnover records on their centralized servers, your core commercial and patient data resides locally on your counter device in an encrypted local database. We do not sell, rent, or harvest your business turnover, customer records, or patient prescription history.

2. Information We Collect

  • Website Demo Requests: When you submit a lead form on our website, we collect your Name, Store Name, Business Category, Mobile Number, City, and Current Billing Setup to fulfill your live demo.
  • Software Terminal Activation: When activating a desktop terminal license, our licensing engine records a cryptographic hardware hash (CPU/Motherboard fingerprint) and IP address for anti-piracy validation.
  • Website Analytics: We use anonymized Google Analytics (GA4) with IP masking to understand general website traffic without tracking personally identifiable individual actions.

3. Customer & Patient Data Ownership

All patient medical records, Schedule H/H1 narcotic registers, farmer Aadhaar subsidy receipts, and invoice records created by your store belong solely and exclusively to you (the licensee). Bill 2 Bharat employees have zero backdoor access to your local machine database unless you explicitly initiate a remote support session via AnyDesk or TeamViewer.

4. WhatsApp Integration & Third-Party Services

When you dispatch WhatsApp receipts to your customers, communications pass directly through the WhatsApp Business API or Web protocol in compliance with Meta's terms. Webhook forms on our landing site are processed via secure SSL endpoints (Web3Forms/Formspree) directly to Founder Pranav Dhanwate's verified email.
